# Findings for the Financial Manager

**From:** Adnaan Ismail, BI Analyst and Developer
**Covers:** March 2024 to August 2026 (FY25, FY26 and the first half of FY27)

Every figure below comes from the new warehouse. Sales, Gross Profit and EBITDA agree with finance's signed-off totals to the cent for all three periods.

## 1. About half of this year's growth is new stores

Sales for March to August 2026 are up 19.6% on last year, which is R1.34m. The nine stores that traded in both periods grew 10.2% and account for R0.64m of it. The other R0.70m is Ballito and Hilton, less the sales lost when Northdale closed.

**Action:** report like-for-like growth next to total growth every month. 10% is the real growth rate of the business.

## 2. Musgrave has a cost problem

Musgrave sells well (R1.29m in FY26) but made only R12,000 EBITDA. Wages take 32% of its sales against about 21% elsewhere, and rent takes 14% against about 9%. This has been the case in all three years.

**Action:** review the staff roster against trading hours, and ask what the lease allows at renewal. Bringing wages to the group level would add about R140,000 a year.

## 3. Pinetown has a discount problem

Pinetown has the third-highest sales in the group, but its Gross Profit is 50.4% against about 56% elsewhere. It gives away 12% of sales in discounts, where every other store gives under 1%. All four cashiers discount at the same rate, so this looks like a standing store practice and not one person. It cost about R206,000 of margin in FY26.

**Action:** find out who authorised the discount and why. If there is no good reason, stop it.

## 4. Margin dipped in April to June 2025 and has not fully recovered

Supplier costs rose about 8% on 1 April 2025. Selling prices only went up on 1 July 2025. For those three months Gross Profit fell from 57.1% to 53.7%, which cost about R108,000. Since July 2025 it has held at 56.0%, still one point below where it was.

**Action:** review selling prices in the same month that supplier costs change. Decide whether the remaining one point should be recovered through price.

## 5. The data supports closing Northdale

Northdale missed its sales budget by 15% to 23% in every month on record. Its sales for March to September fell 24% between 2024 and 2025. It made R108,000 EBITDA in FY25, then lost R3,700 over its last seven months, with a loss in four of them.

**Action:** none needed. The decision was sound.

## 6. Weekly KPIs for operations

Built into the report and defined in the README: Transactions, Average Ticket, Discount %, Void % and Drink Attach % by store by week, plus Labour % by month. Discount % would have flagged Pinetown immediately.

**Action:** review these in the Monday ops meeting. Query any store above 2% discount or 25% labour.

## One question for you

The Gross Profit budget assumes a margin of about 62%. The group has never been above 57.2%, so Gross Profit shows as 10% to 12% under budget every year even though sales are within 3%. Is 62% a deliberate target, or should the budget be reset?
